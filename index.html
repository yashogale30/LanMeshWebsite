Works on the same local network. Free and open.

With LanMesh you can chat, call and share with everyone around you. Classrooms, hostels, hackathons, campsites and blackouts included.

Never miss a moment with voice and video calls

From a quick one to one call to a group room of up to six people, talk face to face over your local network. Media flows directly between devices.

Learn more

Stay on your network

There is no cloud in the middle. Every connection between devices is secured with TLS 1.3 and each device gets its own cryptographic identity. Your messages never leave your local network.

TLS 1.3 transportNo central serverLocal history only

Note: messages relayed across hops are not end to end encrypted yet.

Keep in touch with your groups

Spin up a channel in seconds, like general, dev or wwe. Each group is isolated, so messages and calls stay with the people who joined it.

Say what you feel

Record a voice message for a quick hello, drop in photos, or send any file up to 8 MB. React with an emoji, edit what you wrote, or delete it.

Connect your way

Peers on the same WiFi show up automatically. If your router blocks discovery, scan a QR code or paste a peer address and you are in.

Reach farther with multi hop

Messages hop from device to device, so people out of direct range still get the conversation. Loops are suppressed automatically.
