Stay on your network

There is no cloud in the middle. Every connection between devices is secured with TLS 1.3 and each device gets its own cryptographic identity. Your messages never leave your local network.

TLS 1.3 transportNo central serverLocal history only

Keep in touch with your groups

Spin up a channel in seconds, like general, dev or exams. Each group is isolated, so messages and calls stay with the people who joined it.
